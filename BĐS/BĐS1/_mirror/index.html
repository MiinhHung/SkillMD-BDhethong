FAQ

Question & answer

What makes our interior design agency stand out from the rest?

One team holds the drawing, the budget and the build, so nothing is lost at a handover. Architecture and interiors are resolved together rather than styled on at the very end.

Can I submit unlimited interior design requests?

Yes. Send as many as you like and we work through the queue in the order you set. One request stays active at a time, so you always know what the studio is drawing this week.

What if I only need interior design help for one project?

That is absolutely fine. Single-project work is quoted on its own scope with a fixed fee and a clear end date, and nothing obliges you to carry on once it is handed over.

Can I really request unlimited interior design services?

Yes, genuinely. Across a month there is no cap on how many you send. We keep just one in progress at a time so each gets full attention instead of everything moving slowly.

How quickly will I get my interior design concepts?

First concepts land within five working days of the brief being signed off, and revisions come back in two. You see every stage rather than a single reveal at the very end.

Are there any limits to the number of interior design requests I can make?

No hard limit on the total. Your plan sets how many run at once, one on the studio tier and two on the practice tier, and you can reorder the queue whenever priorities shift.
